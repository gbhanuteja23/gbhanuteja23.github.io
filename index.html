Opti-Flow AI Agent — NVIDIA GSI Hackathon Finalist

Bridging mathematical optimization with spatial clarity for real-world automated logistics. Selected as Top 20 Finalists out of 350+ teams and presented live at NVIDIA India Headquarters, Bangalore.

Top 20 Finalist (Out of 350 Teams) Team AutoRoute Agents (EY-GDS) NVIDIA India HQ, Bangalore  |  Sept 2026

Empowering Warehouse Operators with Real-Time Agentic Replanning & 3D Digital Twins

In just three weeks, our team conceptualized and developed Opti-Flow AI Agent to address real-world industrial warehouse bottlenecks like urgent orders, dynamic priority shifts, and blocked aisles. We engineered a seamless two-tier architecture unifying lightweight operator visualization with high-fidelity physics validation, GPU-accelerated route solving, and natural language multi-agent orchestration.

Tier 1: React + Three.js UI

Lightweight browser frontend providing immediate 3D warehouse layout visualization. Operators evaluate pick capacities, simulate blocked aisles in real-time, and verify route changes interactively before committing to physical execution.

Tier 2: Omniverse & Isaac Sim

Custom NVIDIA Omniverse Extension driving Isaac Sim for high-fidelity physics validation. Stages photorealistic digital twin environments and simulates robotic Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs / Clearpath Ridgeback Franka).

NVIDIA cuOpt Microservice

Accelerated routing solver running locally on cluster port 8000. Resolves complex warehouse picking routes, multi-picker scheduling, and dynamic traveling salesperson heuristics in milliseconds rather than minutes.

NIM & Nemotron 3 Nano

Powers natural language supervisory planning requests. Uses bounded tool calling (get_warehouse_state, propose_picking_plan) to dynamically execute agentic commands while preventing invalid states.

B300 High-Performance GPU Cluster Deployment

Both the NVIDIA cuOpt microservice and the Nemotron 3 Nano Omni models operated entirely locally on the high-performance NVIDIA B300 GPU cluster provided by the organizers, delivering near-instantaneous replanning, ultra-low latency spatial inference, and smooth multi-agent orchestration.

The Architectural Takeaway
“Effective industrial AI isn't simply about solving an optimization algorithm in a black box. It's about bridging mathematical precision with spatial clarity — giving operators interactive, explainable, and human-in-the-loop control over complex automated environments.”
