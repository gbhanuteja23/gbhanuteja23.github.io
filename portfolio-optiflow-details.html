Opti-Flow AI Agent: Real-Time Dynamic Warehouse Spatial Optimization & Multi-Agent Simulation

We are thrilled to announce that our team, AutoRoute Agents, was selected as one of the top 20 finalists out of 350 teams who registered for the NVIDIA - GSI Hackathon. It was an absolute privilege to present our solution, Opti-Flow AI Agent, live at the NVIDIA India headquarters in Bangalore.

#NVIDIA #GSIHackathon #AgenticAI #cuOpt #Nemotron3Nano #NvidiaNIM #IsaacSim #Omniverse #EY

The Challenge

In automated high-throughput warehouses, unexpected disruptions are daily occurrences:

  • Sudden priority rushes requiring immediate reordering of pick tasks.
  • Blocked aisles due to spills, equipment malfunctions, or physical obstacles.
  • Heuristic route algorithms operating as opaque black boxes that leave operators unable to review or verify path validity before triggering robots.
  • High-latency recalculations causing idling fleets and warehouse gridlock.

The Goal

Empower warehouse operators to dynamically adapt to disruptions in real time by:

  • Evaluating pick capacities and dynamically shifting vehicle schedules on the fly.
  • Handling priority shifts interactively with natural language commands.
  • Verifying 3D route changes across intuitive digital layouts before pushing to physical execution.
  • Running end-to-end physics validation in simulation to prevent real-world robot collisions.

Two-Tier Architecture & NVIDIA Tech Stack

In just three weeks, we conceptualized and built two powerful tools to address real-world challenges, combining browser spatial telemetry with high-fidelity physics simulation and GPU-accelerated microservices:

Tier 1: Lightweight React + Three.js UI

Provides immediate operator visualization on any web browser. Supervisors view real-time 3D warehouse layouts, trigger synthetic obstacle simulations (e.g., closing Aisle A3-S), inspect graph-based routing paths, and review proposals with a human-in-the-loop review mechanism.

Tier 2: Omniverse Extension & Isaac Sim

A custom NVIDIA Omniverse Extension driving Isaac Sim for high-fidelity physics validation. Stages photorealistic USD warehouse environments and simulates robotic Autonomous Mobile Robots (Clearpath Ridgeback Franka AMR), validating collision-free navigation and payload constraints.

NVIDIA cuOpt Microservice

GPU-accelerated routing optimization microservice running on cluster port 8000. Resolves complex warehouse picking routes, multi-picker capacity schedules, and vehicle stop orders in sub-second intervals, replacing slow traditional CPU solvers.

NVIDIA NIM & Nemotron 3 Nano Omni

Interprets natural language supervisor planning requests into structured JSON tool calls (get_warehouse_state, propose_picking_plan). Ensures the agent cannot approve or dispatch orders without human-in-the-loop operator confirmation.

Local Deployment on NVIDIA B300 GPU Cluster

Both the NVIDIA cuOpt microservice and the Nemotron 3 Nano Omni models ran entirely locally on the high-performance NVIDIA B300 GPU cluster provided by the organizers, delivering near-instantaneous replanning, ultra-low latency spatial inference, and smooth multi-agent orchestration.

The Core Takeaway
“Effective industrial AI isn't simply about solving an optimization algorithm in a black box. It's about bridging mathematical precision with spatial clarity — giving operators interactive, explainable, and human-in-the-loop control over complex automated environments.”
